## 2) Content Rules (Simple + Safe)

- Use one primary keyword pattern per page type.
- Keep wording natural; avoid repeating exact keyword in every heading.
- Include location naturally in title + H1 + intro for city pages.
- Keep internal links strong:
  - Homepage -> city pages + global search
  - City pages -> daycare detail pages
  - Daycare detail pages -> back to city results
